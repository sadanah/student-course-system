Scenario 1 - Online Food Ordering System

Functional Requirements

Customers
- Create accounts
- Browse available food items
- Search meals by category 
- Add items to cart
- Place food orders (through web/mobile application)
- Secure online payments (credit/debit cards, digital wallets)
- Receive notifications about order status
- Track delivery progress in real time

Managers
- Log into system
- Update food menus
- Add new food item 
- Change prices 
- Remove unavailable items
- Manage customer orders
- Maintain order records
- Generate daily sales report

Delivery riders
- Access delivery management section
- View assigned deliveries
- Accept or reject delivery requests
- Update delivery status (picked up/ on the way/ delivered)


Non-functional requirements
- System should be accessible to many authorized users simultaneously 
- Operate with minimum downtime (less than 0.1%)
- Ensure secure transactions 
- Protect customer information 
- Provide fast responses even in peak hours
- Mobile and desktop responsive 



Scenario 2 - Hospital Management System

Functional Requirements

Patients
- Register
- Log in 
- Book doctor appointments
- View medical reports online

Doctors 
- Access patient medical records
- Update prescriptions
- Maintain treatment details through system

Hospital admins
- Manage doctor and patient information
- Adding new doctors
- Updating schedules
- Handling patient records

Non-Functional Requirements
- Secure system in accordance with GDPR Act for sensitiva data storage and manipulation
- Reliable system interactions 
- Easy to use, even for non-technical people
- Support multiple authorized users simulteneously without delays
- Mobile and Desktop responsive



Scenario 3 - Online Learning Platform

Functional Requirements

Students
- Access learning resources from anywhere through internet
- Log into platform
- Watch lectures
- Download learning materials
- Submit assignments before deadlines

Lecturers
- Upload lecture notes, presentations, tutorial documents, and video lessons.
- Review student submissions
- Provide grades and feedback through platform

Uni Admins
- Separate admin panel to manage user accounts 
- Create, update, and remove student and lecturer accounts


Non-functional Requirements
- Support many authorized users at the same time
- Protect user data securely
- Easy to use platform 
- Available at all times (minimal downtime even with system updates)
- Accessible from computers and mobile devices
